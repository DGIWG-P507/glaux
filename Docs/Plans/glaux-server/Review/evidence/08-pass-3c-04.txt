### 2. F-15 reassessed — command submission without an idempotency key

**Full-section and issue reads:** Guide §4.6 (complete), Guide §6.4 (complete), issue #168 (leaf 5.2.7), issue #174 (leaf 5.3.4).

**Correction to iteration 3.** I said the unkeyed consequence was acknowledged "only obliquely" (L981). That was wrong. `glaux-server-implementation-guide.md:851` states it directly: "Unkeyed submissions remain permitted. After an ambiguous disconnect, matching payloads do not establish retry identity: a new POST may be a distinct action… unique client recovery is not guaranteed and automatic resubmission is unsafe." My grep did not include "unkeyed"; lesson re-recorded.

**Existing documented limitation (design decision, recorded).**
- Guide L505 ("optional… Do not require this header for all CSAPI clients"), L503 ("deliberately does not adopt IDR-029/031's stronger mandatory-header policy"), §6.4 L849–851 (same-key rejoin; unkeyed limits), §8.2 L981 (test scenario).
- Issue #168 (5.2.7): "Do not require Idempotency-Key, deduplicate unkeyed requests by equal parameter values…"; acceptance requires "two unkeyed equal-valued requests remain distinct actions."
- Issue #174 (5.3.4): "Do not make Idempotency-Key mandatory, introduce content-based command deduplication or promise exactly-once physical effects"; deliverable includes "document unkeyed recovery limits"; verification: "document that an ambiguous disconnect cannot be safely resolved by automatic unkeyed resubmission."

**Operational risk retained (accepted by design).** A third-party CSAPI client with no Glaux extension, on a DDIL link, loses a command POST response; its only safe recoveries are "ordinary authorized discovery" (listing commands) or operator reconciliation, and a resubmission may dispatch twice. Research recommended the opposite for physical-effect submissions: IDR-029 §8.2 and rec 4, IDR-031 rec 4, IDR-037 §7.3 ("The unsafe POST profile requires a scoped idempotency key").

**Residual gap.** §12.2 L1118 and §13 record the `If-Match` departure but not this one; the rationale (standard-client interoperability) is implied, not stated.

**Revised:** Severity **Low–Medium** (Medium operational consequence, Low documentation gap); type: accepted credible risk. **Smallest correction:** one §13 row naming IDR-029/031/037's mandatory-key recommendation, the interoperability rationale, and the accepted consequence. **Separate from the finding:** a per-deployment or per-ControlStream option to require the key is a *new feature proposal* that #168/#174 expressly exclude from those leaves; it needs an owner decision and a Guide/Roadmap change, not a review correction. Optional low-cost improvement: Glaux-published client examples always send `Idempotency-Key` on command POST (parallel to L503's "encourage conditional updates in examples").

### 3. F-16 reassessed — batch atomicity

**Reads:** Guide §4.6 complete; issue #104 (3.2.7); `idr-srv-029-transaction-consistency-idempotency-and-concurrency-strategy-report.md:318-322`.

IDR-029 §10 permits three alternatives — "atomic entire batch; independent item transactions with per-item results; or durable asynchronous job" — provided success is never returned while dropping items. The Guide (`glaux-server-implementation-guide.md:507`) selects the first and labels it "a Glaux atomicity choice, not a new OGC batch claim"; #104 selects the same ("all-or-nothing admission is the selected Glaux policy"), excludes partial-success semantics, requires "the source-defined response rather than assuming a single-item Location contract", and requires the choice be documented "separately from normative CSAPI obligations". IDR-031 rec 7 preferred per-item atomicity, but that preference sits inside IDR-029's permitted set, and the Guide/leaf record the selection. **F-16 withdrawn** — verified consistent.
