### 2. Corrections to iteration 9

1. **Failure spool.** I wrote that Guide L600 "matches IDR-041's minimum slice (atomic journal append; independent failure spool)". Wrong: the Guide specifies atomic audit commit only (`glaux-server-implementation-guide.md:501`, `glaux-server-implementation-guide.md:602`). It contains no independent audit path, spool, gap record or failure-class table. Its only non-transactional audit statement is "Record relevant denials safely without turning every denied request into unbounded database load."
2. **Integrity layers.** I wrote that hash-chain/checkpoint layers were "conditional even in the research". Wrong: IDR-041 §11.1 states "All profiles implement Layers 1–3", and Layer 3 is "deterministic canonical event encoding, local sequence, previous-event hash and explicit segment/checkpoint records" — required in every research profile, including local development (§6.3 "Local chain/checkpoint self-test"). Only Layer 4 (separated MAC/signature) is expose-and-declare, Layer 5 (independent custody) is deployment-selected, and WORM / per-record signatures are conditional (§19.2).
3. **Line anchors.** My §4.10 citations in iterations 7–9 were off by one to two lines. Correct anchors: L594 JWT; L596 development identities/TLS/CORS; L598 authorize-before-counts; L600 schema-true projection; **L602 audit**; L604 production context; L606 uploader/creator roles; L610 labeling. Cumulative notes now use these.

### 3. IDR-041 — what the research recommended versus what the Guide adopted

**Adopted (design text present):**
- Audit as a durable server-owned record, not filtered telemetry: L602 "Diagnostic logs may be sampled; committed write/command accountability cannot depend on them"; `glaux-server-implementation-guide.md:527` "command evidence cannot be reconstructed from sampled logs". (IDR-041 §1, §19.2.)
- E3 — minimum audit in the same transaction as state/revision/outbox: L501; Roadmap 1.3.3; #15 requires injected-failure rollback and "supplied actor/source rather than only record counts".
- E4 — durable pre-effect evidence for commands: `glaux-server-implementation-guide.md:574` "Persist immutable command intent/revisions and an adapter-work record before dispatch"; `glaux-server-implementation-guide.md:845`; #163.
- Minimization: no secrets, tokens, raw sensitive payloads or protected policy reasons (L602). Field floor: verified actor/source, operation, target/revision, time, outcome, correlation identifier (L602; §6.1 `glaux-server-implementation-guide.md:709`).
- Role separation (uploader ≠ creator; delegation verification state retained): L606. Private audit records are not CSAPI families: `glaux-server-implementation-guide.md:713`. Exchange resolution audited with the revision: `glaux-server-implementation-guide.md:641`.
- Claim language: "A mandatory tamper-proof ledger… is not proposed" (L602) is consistent with IDR-041 §11.3/§19.2, which themselves reject "tamper-proof" and "non-repudiation".

**Not adopted (Guide silent):** Layer 2 ordinary prevention (separate schema, append-only interface, writer role without UPDATE/DELETE); Layer 3 local detection (canonical bytes, per-stream sequence, previous-hash, checkpoints); independent E2 path/durable spool/gap events/reserved capacity; audit search/review/annotation/export and audit-of-audit; audit retention class; taxonomy/schema versioning of audit records; DDIL node/epoch/clock-quality fields. The §12.2 deliberate-non-adoption list (`glaux-server-implementation-guide.md:1118`) does not mention any of these; the only recorded decision is the "tamper-proof ledger" sentence, which declines a stronger claim than the accepted research actually made.

Applying the standing rule: none of these is automatically a missing requirement. The Goal (§5.7) requires "explicit treatment of… access governance, failure semantics" and "governance of authorized command interactions"; it does not require tamper-evidence or any audit architecture. The test I applied is whether a difference leaves the Guide unable to support its **own** accountability claims.

### 4. Findings from this iteration

**F-19 — Audit record has no stated immutability or assurance level.** Credible risk + documentation gap; Low–Medium.
- *Evidence:* §6.1 row "Server audit" (`glaux-server-implementation-guide.md:709`) has no update rule, whereas sibling rows do ("Immutable source bytes" L702; "Changes create accountable revisions" L698; migrations "immutable SQL files" L529). §4.10 L602 says "durable" only. §4.7 does not say whether a configured retention policy may remove audit rows. #15 verifies that audit facts are written and roll back, not that they cannot later be changed. IDR-041 §11.1 (Layers 1–3 in all profiles), §13.2 (writer role without update/delete) and §19.1 items 3 and 6 were accepted 15 Sep 2026; the Guide neither adopts nor records declining them.
- *Failure scenario:* the serving database role that inserts audit rows in the shared transaction will, by default, also hold UPDATE/DELETE on them; a defect, injection (which the Guide tests for at L612), compromised application credentials, or a retention job can alter or remove evidence with no trace, while operators read "durable audit" as stronger than it is.
- *Contrary evidence checked:* the Guide's immutability for revisions is also application-level, so this is consistent treatment if stated; the "tamper-proof ledger" disclaimer is honest about what is not provided; Goal §5.7 does not mandate integrity protection.
- *Smallest correction:* one sentence in §4.10 or the §6.1 row: audit records are append-only through every application path; the serving role should not hold UPDATE/DELETE on audit tables where the deployment can grant separately; state whether retention applies to audit rows. Record in §12.2/§13 that IDR-041's hash-linked streams/checkpoints are deferred, with the resulting assurance statement (durability and atomicity, not tamper-evidence).
- *Resolving test:* in 1.3.3/#15, an attempted update/delete of an audit row through the application/serving role fails and leaves the row unchanged.
- *Timing:* before #15 merges (the audit table migration is where grants and rules are set). Adopting Layer 3 remains an owner option, not a correction.

**F-20 — "Record relevant denials safely" has no owner, scope, or failure behaviour.** Unresolved question / coverage gap; Low.
- *Evidence:* Guide L602. Roadmap grep for `denial|denied|audit` finds no leaf whose Done criterion requires a denial record; #22's verification text says "with allowance for safe denial auditing" (tolerates, does not require); #163 requires only that denied targets "leave no admitted work". "Relevant" is undefined; behaviour when the denial write fails is unstated (IDR-041 §12.1 E2: the denial stands, evidence goes through an independent path, otherwise a declared gap).
- *Failure scenario:* an authenticated caller's unauthorized command or cross-source publish attempt leaves no evidence; or an implementer records every anonymous 401/404 and creates the load the Guide warns against.
- *Contrary evidence:* the Guide does not claim denial-evidence completeness, and the denial itself is fail-closed regardless.
- *Smallest correction:* define "relevant" (authenticated caller denied a mutation, command or subscription; aggregate repeated authentication failures; no per-request storage for anonymous read denials), name the owning leaf, and state that the denial response never depends on the record succeeding.
- *Timing:* before #22 — can be combined with the F-03 correction, which touches the same section and issue.

**No finding:** offline audit continuity (IDR-041 §15) is inherent in the single-store design — the database is on the node; node/epoch/clock-quality audit fields are research-optional. E4 for commands is satisfied by L574/L845.
